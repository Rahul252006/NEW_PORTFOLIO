Overview & Business Scope

As the adoption of electric vehicles (EVs) accelerates globally, estimating vehicle market value based on performance and battery specifications has become critical for automotive manufacturers, dealerships, and buyers.

Electric vehicle pricing is driven by key engineering specifications:

  • Powertrain Performance: Battery Capacity (kWh), Top Speed (km/h), 0–100 Acceleration (sec)
  • Efficiency & Range: Driving Range (km), Efficiency (Wh/km), Fast Charging Speed (km/h)
  • Utility & Configuration: Seating Capacity, Drive Configuration (AWD/RWD/FWD), Towing Capacity
"Supervised regression modeling translates non-linear battery and performance specifications into reliable market price estimates, empowering data-driven pricing strategies."

Key Findings & Feature Importance

Feature importance analysis from the Random Forest model revealed the strongest determinants of electric vehicle valuation:

  • Battery Capacity & Driving Range: Top primary drivers of vehicle price due to direct battery pack component costs.
  • Fast Charging Speed & Efficiency: High fast-charging rates and lower Wh/km consumption correlated strongly with premium pricing tiers.
  • Acceleration & Top Speed: Dual-motor performance configurations created noticeable price premiums.
