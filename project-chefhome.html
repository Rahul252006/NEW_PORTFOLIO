ChefHome — Home Chef Booking Platform

A platform that connects customers with professional home chefs, enabling users to book personalized cooking services directly at their homes.

Role
Sole Developer

Category
Full-Stack Service Platform

Why I Built It

I noticed that many people prefer freshly prepared food at home for special occasions, family gatherings, and personal dietary preferences. However, finding reliable chefs often happens through word-of-mouth recommendations and lacks a streamlined booking process.

I built ChefHome to create a platform that simplifies chef discovery, booking, and communication while providing a more convenient experience for both customers and chefs.

The Problem & Solution

Problem: The home-chef industry is largely unorganized. Customers struggle to find trusted chefs, compare options, and manage bookings efficiently.

Solution: ChefHome centralizes the entire process by allowing users to discover chefs, review their profiles, and book services through a single platform. This creates a smoother experience while improving visibility for chefs.

The Core Systems (Features)

  • Chef Discovery: Allows users to browse and explore available chefs based on their preferences.
  • Online Booking System: Provides a structured workflow for scheduling chef services.
  • User Profiles: Enables users to manage bookings and personal information.
  • Secure Payment Integration: Supports digital payment processing for a smoother booking experience.
  • Responsive Design: Optimized for both desktop and mobile users to ensure accessibility across devices.

Outcomes & Key Learnings

  • Product Thinking: Learned how technology can address inefficiencies in service-based industries.
  • Full-Stack Development: Strengthened my understanding of application architecture, user workflows, and database-driven systems.
  • User-Centered Design: Recognized the importance of building intuitive experiences that reduce friction for users.
  • Real-World Problem Solving: Gained experience identifying market gaps and designing solutions around actual customer needs.
