Why I Built It

As a student, I noticed that important academic and career resources were scattered across dozens of different websites. Students often switch between AI tools, resume checkers, placement portals, previous year papers, FFCS planners, CGPA calculators, and company career pages just to manage their academic journey.

This fragmented experience wastes time and creates unnecessary friction. I built VITLY to bring these resources together into a single platform that helps students learn, prepare, plan, and grow more efficiently. My goal was to reduce the number of tools students need and create a platform specifically designed around their daily needs.

The Problem & Solution

Students rely on multiple disconnected platforms throughout their academic journey:

  • AI tools for learning
  • Separate websites for resume reviews
  • Individual company portals for job opportunities
  • Academic portals for previous year papers
  • External websites for CGPA calculations
  • Manual planning for FFCS course registration

Managing all these resources separately creates an inefficient student experience.

"Instead of switching between multiple websites, students can access AI-powered guidance, academic resources, career opportunities, planning tools, and performance tracking from a single dashboard."

VITLY centralizes these resources into one platform, creating a smoother, faster, and more productive experience specifically designed around student workflows.

The Core Systems (Features)

  • AI Assistant: A custom AI-powered student assistant designed to provide academic guidance, answer queries, and assist students throughout their learning journey — built specifically around student workflows and educational use cases.
  • Resume Score Checker: Analyzes resumes and provides a score along with actionable feedback to help students identify weaknesses and improve their chances during internship and placement applications.
  • Company Career Directory: Aggregates career pages from companies into one searchable location, allowing students to discover opportunities without manually visiting individual company websites.
  • Previous Year Question Papers: Provides easy access to previous year examination papers, helping students prepare more effectively and understand exam patterns.
  • FFCS Planner: Helps students plan and organize their course registrations by visualizing schedules and avoiding timetable conflicts during semester enrollment.
  • CGPA Calculator: Allows students to calculate their CGPA quickly and accurately while tracking academic performance throughout their degree.
  • Student-Centric Dashboard: A centralized dashboard that brings together academic tools, career resources, and AI-powered assistance into a single experience.
  • Responsive Experience: Designed to work seamlessly across desktop and mobile devices, ensuring accessibility wherever students need it.

Outcomes & Key Learnings

  • Building Real Student Solutions: Learned how to identify genuine student pain points and convert them into practical software solutions.
  • Product Design: Developed a deeper understanding of designing products around user workflows rather than individual features.
  • AI Integration: Gained experience integrating AI capabilities into a platform built for real-world educational use cases.
  • Full-Stack Development: Strengthened my skills in designing scalable systems, managing data, and connecting multiple services into a unified platform.
  • User Experience Thinking: Learned that reducing friction is often more valuable than adding new features. Bringing multiple student resources into one place significantly improves usability and convenience.
